The Rig — NetCoin Economy Tuner

Editable balance tool for Rig_DD.md §4–§7. Tweak any number or part below — the summary, graphs, and affordability table recompute live. All values are a proposed baseline; nothing here is final.

Three player archetypes are simulated: Grinder (daily + overclock), Regular (daily, no overclock), Casual (every other day → streak keeps resetting).

1 · Economy parameters

Idle mining
Rig fills to the cap, then stops — the full tank is the hook to return & collect.
Daily payout & streak
Missing a day resets the streak to day 1 (strict).
Overclock (using applets)
No direct NetCoin per applet — engagement boosts the rate instead.
Recurring milestones
Re-arm off the same streak: 7/14/21… and 30/60/90…
Achievements
Front-loaded & tapering — achievements are the only direct NetCoin source besides idle/daily.
Jack-In (gambling)
Stake-only cash-out (free house chips never convert). House edge = net drain on average; cap bounds the lucky tail.
Simulation
Archetypes auto-reinvest earnings into parts (cheapest first) so the rig compounds.
